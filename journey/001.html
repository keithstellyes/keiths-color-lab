Let's start with the basics. 1 bit. What can we do with 1 bit color? But how do we do that? Let's try a simple naive algorithm. For this case, we assume our pixels are three-tuples of red, green, blue in the range 0.0 ... 1.0. It's what graphics code sometimes like.

Naive 1-bit grayscale
Every pixel is either white or black based on a given in pixel.
\(y = \frac{in.red + in.green + in.blue}{3} \)
output pixel \(=\) \(\begin{cases} white & y \geq 0.5 \\ black & y \leq 0.5 \end{cases}\)

There's a few ways we can improve this.

  • 1. Red, green, and blue are not all just as sensitive to human eyes.
  • 2. We can try dithering to simulate colors in-between
    • Let's go through these one-by-one.
